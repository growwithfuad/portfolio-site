My story

I'm Fuad Hasan, from Bangladesh. I work with Australian dental clinics, focusing on implants and cosmetic dentistry — the treatments where a single patient decision carries real weight, and where advertising has to be careful as well as effective.

I hold Google Ads Search certification. But most of what I know about dental marketing didn't come from a course. It came from reading clinic websites the way a nervous patient reads them at midnight: Is the price here? Can I trust these people? What happens if I tap this button? The gaps I kept finding — between the ad and the page, between the promise and the proof — became my whole method.

I also run a small seasonal fruit business with my family back home. It taught me the same lesson from the other side of the counter: if the fruit isn't ripe, you don't call it ripe. Honesty isn't a marketing angle. It's just how business survives.

How I think

Three rules run everything I do — audits, campaigns, and every message I send.

Observation before hypothesis

What I can see from the outside is an observation. What I suspect is a hypothesis. I label them separately, and I never present the second as the first.

Research first, always

Business, landing page, ad, competitor, opportunity — in that order, before any opinion. Five to ten minutes of looking beats an hour of guessing.

No claim without data

Without account access, I don't claim what's happening inside a campaign. Public observation is step one of proof — I work from there, honestly.
