Where campaigns usually break

After studying clinic after clinic, the same three breaks appear again and again. Most of the time, the break is in one of these three places — not in the budget.

01 · The Search

The wrong intent behind the click — someone looking for prices or a general check-up, not a treatment.

02 · The Landing Page

The ad promises one thing and the page says another. The ad and the page should finish each other's sentences.

03 · The Promise

Offers that sound like guarantees. In Australian dental advertising, trust is built without them.
