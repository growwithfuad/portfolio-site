The problem this answers

Most clinics don't lose patients in the ad account. They lose them in the gap between the ad and the page — where nobody is looking.

The ad and the page tell different stories

The ad talks about one treatment or offer; the page it lands on talks about something else. The patient feels the mismatch in seconds and leaves.

Prices and offers are hard to find

A patient comparing two clinics will choose the one that answers the price question. If your page hides it, the comparison happens on someone else's page.

The details don't line up

Different phone numbers on different pages. Ads running at hours when nobody can answer. Reviews you have earned that never appear on your own site. Small breaks, real cost.

What the audit checks — 10 points

Every audit follows the same checklist, so nothing depends on my mood that day. Observation first: what can be seen from the outside, checked in order.

1

Message match

Does the landing page continue the exact promise the ad made — same treatment, same words?

2

Price & offer clarity

Are prices and offers visible, specific, and consistent everywhere they appear?

3

Trust signals

Credentials, experience and proof a patient can verify — presented where decisions are made.

4

AHPRA-aware wording

Language that builds trust without crossing Australian dental advertising rules.

5

Booking path

How many steps — and how much friction — between landing on the page and booking?

6

Phone consistency

One number, the same everywhere: site, ads, and listings. No dead ends.

7

Review visibility

Are the reviews you have earned actually shown on your own pages?

8

Ad schedule

Do your ads run when your team can answer — or all night, unanswered?

9

Page speed

Does the page load fast enough on a phone, where most patients first see it?

10

Call to action

Is the next step obvious, singular, and easy to take on any device?
